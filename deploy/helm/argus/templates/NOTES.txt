Engine API (in-cluster):
  http://{{ include "argus.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc:{{ .Values.service.port }}

OTLP gRPC receiver for the sampled mirror (point a second exporter here):
  {{ include "argus.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc:{{ .Values.engine.otlp.grpcPort }}
It has no authentication: the NetworkPolicy (networkPolicy.otlpAllowedNamespaces)
decides who may send to it, on a CNI that enforces NetworkPolicy.

Every /api/ call needs this bearer token (/healthz and /metrics do not):
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get secret {{ include "argus.fullname" . }} -o jsonpath='{.data.api-token}' | base64 -d

Put the engine URL and the token on the Argus app's configuration page in
Grafana (Administration > Plugins > Argus). The plugin's health check says
whether the engine accepts the token.
